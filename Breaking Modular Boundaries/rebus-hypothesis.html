What Is REBUS?

REBUS stands for Relaxed Beliefs Under Psychedelics. It is a theoretical framework proposing that psychedelics may temporarily reduce the influence of some strongly held beliefs and expectations. Carhart-Harris and Friston (2019) developed REBUS as a model for understanding how psychedelics may alter the brain's usual patterns of processing.

Predictive Processing

Predictive processing describes how the brain uses prior expectations to interpret incoming information. REBUS proposes that psychedelic states may loosen the influence of established expectations, making thinking feel less rigid and allowing new interpretations to be considered. This is a proposed account of psychedelic effects, not a confirmed description of every person's experience (Carhart-Harris & Friston, 2019).

Therapeutic Implications

The essay connects REBUS to the possibility that people may reconsider recurring thought patterns associated with conditions such as depression and anxiety. It also discusses ego dissolution as a potentially meaningful experience during psychedelic-assisted therapy. These ideas may help guide research, but they do not show that REBUS or ego dissolution causes recovery. REBUS remains a theoretical model, and the therapeutic mechanisms are still under study.
