Research integrity note

The clinical studies by Goodwin et al. and Griffiths et al. investigate psilocybin rather than DMT. They provide broader context for psychedelic-assisted therapy, not direct proof of DMT's clinical effectiveness. Timmermann et al. (2023) examine DMT-related brain activity using EEG-fMRI; these neuroimaging findings do not establish clinical effectiveness. REBUS, described by Carhart-Harris and Friston (2019), is a theoretical model, not a confirmed explanation of how psychedelic therapy works.
