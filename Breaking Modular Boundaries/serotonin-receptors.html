Introduction to Serotonin

Serotonin is a neurotransmitter involved in numerous neurological processes, including mood, cognition, perception, and sleep. Psychedelics such as DMT interact with particular serotonin receptors rather than simply increasing serotonin throughout the brain.

The 5-HT2A Receptor

The serotonin 5-HT2A receptor is an important target of classic psychedelic compounds. DMT's interaction with this receptor is considered a key part of its effects and can alter neural signaling and communication between brain regions (Barker, 2018; Timmermann et al., 2023). Neuroimaging research examines these changes during the DMT experience.

Why It Matters

Studying receptor activity helps researchers investigate how psychedelic compounds influence perception, cognition, and brain function. The essay connects 5-HT2A activity with changes in thought patterns that researchers are studying in psychedelic-assisted therapy. Models such as REBUS propose that psychedelics may temporarily loosen strongly held beliefs, but receptor activation alone does not show that treatment will be effective (Carhart-Harris & Friston, 2019).
